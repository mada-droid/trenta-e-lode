## Focus-mode backgrounds

The six 120-second animated scenes are published as assets of the GitHub release `backgrounds-v1` and downloaded on first use. They were animated with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/) (code in `video-loops/`) on top of illustrations:

- *Sunset hill*, *Village by the river*, *Winter village · aurora*, *Sunken forest*: illustrations generated by the project author with Google Gemini.
- *Sakura garden · night* and *Spring temple · castle in the clouds*: based on wallpapers collected by the project author (the sakura and castle illustrations carry the signature "AFERA XV"). If you are the artist and want them credited differently or removed, please open an issue.
